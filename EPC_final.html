About the Map

This map visualizes spatial patterns of the gap between the UK's current residential energy use and its potential energy use if simple, cost-effective retrofitting were implemented.

By hovering over an area, you can see how much these energy improvements would reduce energy consumption (and costs) and CO2 emissions, both at the household and area level.

Data: EPC Open Data Communities, Digimap (OS Products)
